DETECTION FRAMEWORK
The 7 Signals
Wallet Age
#1
How recently the wallet was created. Fresh wallets trading large are highly suspicious.
Size vs Liquidity
#2
Trade size relative to market liquidity. Large trades in thin markets signal conviction.
Bet Concentration
#3
Portfolio allocation to a single market. All-in bets suggest private information.
Time to Resolution
#4
How close the market is to resolving. Last-minute bets are classic insider behavior.
Win Rate
#5
Historical prediction accuracy. Suspiciously high win rates indicate an edge.
Price Impact
#6
Willingness to accept slippage. Crossing the spread aggressively signals urgency.
Contrarian
#7
Buying low-probability outcomes under 25¢. Contrarian bets that win suggest insider knowledge.
