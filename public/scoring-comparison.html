03 — Differentiation

Unique Signals

Polywatch Only

Wallet Age w: 0.25
Heaviest signal. Fresh/burner wallets score near 100. Detects throwaway accounts created specifically for insider trades.
Bet Concentration w: 0.15
Wallets active in only 1–3 markets score high. Single-purpose wallets are a strong insider indicator.
Win Rate w: 0.10
Historical track record across ≥5 resolved bets. Sustained high win rates are statistically improbable without edge.
Contrarian w: 0.05
Buying outcomes priced below 10–15% implies confidence against market consensus. Potential insider knowledge.
Category Multiplier ×0.5
Sports, esports, and crypto price bets are deprioritized. Insider trading is structurally less likely in these categories.

Claudio's Model Only

Persistence Abnormality w: 0.20
Checks whether the post-trade price move sustained or reverted. A lasting move is stronger evidence of informed trading.
Suppressors hard / soft
Explicit false-positive filters: wide spread illusion, near-resolution noise, duplicate events, public-data markets. Hard suppressors block alerting entirely.
Public Information Layer step 2
Cross-references news articles and public sources. High public info score suppresses the anomaly — the trade was likely reacting to public events, not insider knowledge.

05 — Conclusions

Key Takeaways

Consider Adopting

Public Information Scoring

Cross-referencing news and public sources to suppress trades that are simply reacting to public events. This is the biggest architectural difference and would significantly reduce false positives.

Consider Adopting

Persistence Check

Verifying that the price move held after the trade adds a valuable "was this real?" validation layer. A quick blip that reverts is noise; a sustained move is signal.

Our Advantage

Wallet-Level Signals

Wallet age, bet concentration, and win rate catch burner-wallet patterns that a purely trade-centric model misses entirely. These are our strongest differentiators.

Our Advantage

Broader Signal Coverage

7 sub-scores vs 4 gives wider coverage. Combined with category multipliers, we cast a broader net while still filtering low-value market categories.
